How This Started

I was bored. Just scrolling X, watching the same surface-level AI news over and over, new model drops, benchmark scores(kimi k3 killed fable), the usual hype cycle. Struck my mind that:

i'm just consuming some info superficially, idk what is happening under the hood.

Was having a conversation with a junior(few days back, he asked me to explore inference for fun) and a friend. Gave a bunch of resources, told me to dive into Andrej Karpathy's playlist on YouTube(pretty long, i just referred these).

So I did. Randomly, almost on a whim. I started watching, started coding, started making a plan(hey claude, make me a plan) and yes i'm on hopium that i learn something new and build all of this from scratch, lets see how it goes. v0 plan which i have in my mind is.

build micrograd + understand backprop → build a transformer from scratch → write a GPT tokenizer → build a mini GPT → read around vLLM → eventually, build a mini vLLM.

This blog is the documentation of that journey. Every step, every build, every misunderstanding along the way.
